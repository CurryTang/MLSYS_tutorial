# Style Guidelines & Technical Standards

## Tone and Content Delivery (Strict Direct Knowledge Rule)
- **No Promotional / Clickbait / "Advertisement" Phrasing**:
  - Do NOT include sensationalist phrasing such as "顶级量化真题", "面试宝典", "大厂必考", "原题描述", "秒杀", "防坑指南", "明星级 Alpha".
  - Deliver technical knowledge directly, concisely, and objectively without preamble or hyperbole ("直接进入知识").
  - Focus strictly on mathematical derivations, statistical mechanics, systems architecture, algorithmic complexity, and production-grade code implementations.
