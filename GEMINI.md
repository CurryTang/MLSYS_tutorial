# Style Guidelines & Technical Standards

## Tone and Content Delivery (Strict Direct Knowledge Rule)
- **No Promotional / Clickbait / "Advertisement" Phrasing**:
  - Do NOT include sensationalist phrasing such as "顶级量化真题", "面试宝典", "大厂必考", "原题描述", "秒杀", "防坑指南", "明星级 Alpha".
  - Deliver technical knowledge directly, concisely, and objectively without preamble or hyperbole ("直接进入知识").
  - Focus strictly on mathematical derivations, statistical mechanics, systems architecture, algorithmic complexity, and production-grade code implementations.

## Diagram & Visualization Guidelines (Avoid Gratuitous Diagrams)
- **No Superfluous / Complicating Flowcharts (严禁无实质增量的花哨流程图)**:
  - Do NOT generate gratuitous Mermaid flowcharts or diagrams for simple procedural steps, interview thinking frameworks (e.g., “明确假设 $\to$ 数据治理 $\to$ 检验选型 $\to$ 效应量评估 $\to$ 现实陷阱规避”), or straightforward decision paths.
  - Avoid artificially complicating clear concepts ("切勿为了画图而画图，故意搞复杂").
  - Present logical steps directly and crisply through clean structured text, clear bullet points, or rigorous mathematical derivations.
  - Reserve diagrams strictly for truly complex distributed system architectures, deep network layers, or hardware data paths where visual spatial relationships provide indispensable value.
